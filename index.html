Method & limitations

Required gross floor area = units × net unit size ÷ efficiency. Replacement capacity proxy = sum of lot area × PLUTO residential FAR, not unused FAR or legal entitlement.

Accommodation is capped at 1 for scoring. Removal and assembly use inverse min-max normalization across the complete multi-lot candidates in this snapshot; a constant component ties at 1. Scores are weighted sums × 100, not feasibility percentages. Equal scores share rank.

The detached Dutch Kills creek waterfront does not face Roosevelt Island. It is excluded from the shoreline before generating the 1,000-foot candidate buffer, not just hidden from the map.

All existing residential units are presumed removed. Occupancy, displacement and existing affordability are unknown. Net gains assume the full target is built, not a forecast. Retention adds/removes zero units.

Park conflicts remain visible, not excluded. Zoning, MIH, ownership, approvals, costs and engineering are unresolved. Tax-lot assemblages are not verified legal zoning lots.

This is a mode-neutral redevelopment screen, shared with no-bridge redevelopment. No pedestrian/bike, transit or vehicle bridge benefit has been calculated.

Method & limitations

Precomputed walking results, not live routing. Constant 3 mph; no slope, crossing, stairs or elevator delay. The 554-foot straight bridge and access links are provisional, not engineered or legally approved.

Source node IDs and endpoint levels define connectivity. Shorelines, boundary lines, vehicle-only roads and unbuilt streets are excluded. Sidewalks, crossings, barriers and access hours remain incomplete; this is not a pedestrian navigation service or step-free analysis.

Lines show reachable street intervals, not all reachable land. Node counts are network diagnostics, not population or jobs. Blank travel times mean not reached within 45 minutes.

Roosevelt Island internal routing is provisional. Queens travel times need not improve when a bridge is added. No cycling, transit, vehicle, rent or property-value effects are modeled. Siting rankings are unchanged.

Source & limitations

StreetEasy aggregate series, through September 2026. Native source values are retained: unit metadata remains unverified. No currency, percent, per-square-foot or index-base conversion is applied. Shares remain fractions.

Missing months stay missing; lines break at gaps. Orange points flag suspect source values, including six negative days-on-market cells. Nothing is corrected, dropped or imputed.

Geographies are publisher labels, not verified boundaries, NTAs or community districts. Index series cover only four boroughs; other series include city, borough and neighborhood labels. No neighborhood-to-lot join is implied.

These aggregates do not establish achieved rents, unit-level prices, an affordable/market-rate mix or a causal bridge premium. Historical release dates are unverified; these are not prediction-safe features.
